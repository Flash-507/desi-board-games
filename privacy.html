Privacy policy

Barakatta: Desi Board Games (app ID com.gurutheja.barakatta) · Effective 24 November 2026

Desi Board Games is a box of six board games for families, including children. It is made by an individual developer, Guru Theja Reddy Gopireddy (contact: gurutheja.g@gmail.com). This policy explains what happens to information when you play.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you, unless a grown-up chooses to sign in to back up players (see "Backup and accounts" below). Signing in is never needed to play. The app has no analytics and no tracking by us. We do not sell data. The free game shows a few small ads (see "Ads" below); our ad partner, Google AdMob, handles those ads.

What stays on your device

The game saves your current game, your settings (language, sound, vibration, speed) and whether you finished the tutorial. These are stored only on your phone or tablet. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

From version 0.12 you can add players (Home → Players): a name, a colour, a picture, an age group (under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 and over) and their wins and scores. They are kept only on this phone, are never sent anywhere, and can be changed or deleted at any time; uninstalling the app deletes them. The age group is used only so that the ad after a game suits the youngest person who played.

The player names you type in setup are kept only in the saved game on your device.

Backup and accounts (optional)

From version 0.13 a grown-up can sign in with Google or Apple (Players → Back up players) to keep the players and their scores safe and see them on their other phones. Before signing in, the game asks the year of birth; under 18 it says to ask a grown-up, and the answer is not kept. Children never sign in: they play as players under the grown-up's account.

When you sign in we keep, in Google Firebase (Google processes it for us): your account's email address and name as given by Google or Apple and a user id; the players (name, colour, picture, age group, tips seen) and their scores; a random id for each phone, so games from different phones are counted apart; and whether "Remove ads" was bought. It is used only to back up and sync your players. It is not shared, sold or used for ads. Only your own account can read it.

Deleting it: Players → Back up players → Delete account deletes the backup and the account straight away (the players stay on your phone). You can also ask by email (see delete-account.html); we delete within 30 days. Signing out keeps the backup for next time.

Online play

Online play is added in version 0.6. Earlier versions never connect to the internet. If you choose to play online with friends, the game connects to our game server and sends the nickname you choose and your moves, so the other players in your room can see them. Rooms are identified by a short code. The server keeps this only while the room is open and does not store it afterwards. Online play does not need an account, an email address or a phone number. If you never use online play, the game does not connect to the internet.

Problem reports

From version 0.9 you can choose to send a problem report (Settings or the pause menu, or after the app crashed). A report contains only what you see on the "Report a problem" screen before sending: the kind of problem, an optional note you type, the app version, the phone's system version, language, chosen look and screen size, and, if you leave them on, the game board and throws or the error the app hit, and a small picture of Barakatta's own screen (it can show the player names typed in setup; it never shows anything outside the app). It contains no name, no contact details and no device identifier. Reports are stored in a Google Sheet and Google Drive owned by the developer (Google processes them for us), read only to fix problems, and deleted within 12 months. Nothing is sent unless you press Send. Please don't write personal details in the note.

Ads

The free game may show: a small banner ad in its own strip at the very top of the screen while you play (never over the board), a small banner at the top of the game-over screen, and a full-screen ad after a finished game, at most once every 15 minutes, which can be closed. Every ad is marked "Ad". There are no ads when the app opens, in the middle of a move, in the tutorial, after your very first game, or in online games. Ads come from Google AdMob. The first time, the game asks "What year were you born?"; it keeps only an age group (under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 and over) on your phone, never the year.

  • Under 18: ads are requested as child-directed (under 13) or for teens (13 to 17), are rated for general audiences, and are never personalised. Google does not use the device's advertising ID for these requests.
  • 18 and over: Google may show personalised ads and may use the device's advertising ID and approximate location, as described in Google's advertising policy. Where the law requires it (for example in Europe), Google first asks for your consent. You can reset the advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings.

The developer never receives your advertising ID, location or anything about you from AdMob, only totals of ads shown.

Remove ads

You can remove ads with a one-time purchase in Settings. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store (on a child's phone, they may ask a parent first). The game only learns that "Remove ads" was bought, and remembers it on your phone. "Restore purchase" brings it back on a new phone.

Playing on Wi-Fi

Friends on the same Wi-Fi can play together: one phone hosts the game and the others join by scanning a QR code on the host's screen with their camera. The camera is used only while the scan screen is open, only to read that QR code; no picture is taken, kept or sent anywhere. The game travels directly between the phones on your Wi-Fi; nothing goes to us. While a phone hosts, Android shows a notification and keeps the game running in the background; hosting stops when you tap "Stop hosting", close the app, or after 10 minutes with nobody playing.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game can remind you the next day about a game you left unfinished, and tell you when it's your turn in an online game while the app isn't on screen. Both are made on your phone by the app itself; nothing is sent to us or anyone else to do this. You can turn each one off in Settings, or turn off notifications for Barakatta in your phone's settings.

Children

Desi Board Games is suitable for children. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Players under 18 only ever see non-personalised, family-rated ads; the one in-app purchase (Remove ads) goes through Google Play or the App Store, which can ask a parent first. Online play only shares the nickname a player chooses; we recommend children use a first name or a made-up name.

Permissions

The app may use vibration for game feedback, and internet access for online play, problem reports and ads. The camera is used only to scan a friend's QR code to join a Wi-Fi game (nothing is recorded). Hosting a Wi-Fi game uses Android's foreground-service and keep-awake permissions, with a notification while it runs. The ads and purchase libraries add Android's advertising-ID, network-state and billing permissions. The app does not use your microphone, contacts, precise location or files.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy: gurutheja.g@gmail.com

ఉచిత యాప్‌లో ఇవి కనిపించవచ్చు: ఆడుతున్నప్పుడు తెర పైభాగంలో దాని స్వంత పట్టీలో ఒక చిన్న ప్రకటన (బోర్డు మీద ఎప్పుడూ కాదు), ఆట ముగిసిన తెర పైభాగంలో ఒక చిన్న ప్రకటన, మరియు ఆట ముగిసిన తర్వాత, 15 నిమిషాలకు ఒకసారి మించకుండా, మూసివేయగల పూర్తి-తెర ప్రకటన. ప్రతి ప్రకటనకు "ప్రకటన" అని గుర్తు ఉంటుంది. యాప్ తెరిచినప్పుడు, ఆట మధ్యలో, ట్యుటోరియల్‌లో, మీ మొదటి ఆట తర్వాత, ఆన్‌లైన్ ఆటల్లో ప్రకటనలు ఉండవు. ప్రకటనలు Google AdMob నుండి వస్తాయి. మొదటిసారి ఆట "మీరు ఏ సంవత్సరంలో పుట్టారు?" అని అడుగుతుంది; సంవత్సరాన్ని కాకుండా వయసు వర్గాన్ని (13 లోపు, 13 నుండి 17, 18 పైబడి) మాత్రమే మీ ఫోన్‌లో ఉంచుతుంది. 18 ఏళ్లలోపు వారికి వ్యక్తిగతీకరించని, కుటుంబానికి తగిన ప్రకటనలు మాత్రమే. 18 పైబడిన వారికి Google వ్యక్తిగతీకరించిన ప్రకటనలు చూపవచ్చు; చట్టం కోరిన చోట ముందుగా అనుమతి అడుగుతుంది.

ప్రకటనలు తీసివేయడం

సెట్టింగ్‌లలో ఒక్కసారి కొనుగోలుతో ప్రకటనలు తీసివేయవచ్చు. చెల్లింపును Google Play లేదా App Store పూర్తిగా నిర్వహిస్తాయి (పిల్లల ఫోన్‌లో తల్లిదండ్రుల అనుమతి అడగవచ్చు).

వైఫైలో ఆట

ఒకే వైఫైలో ఉన్న స్నేహితులు కలిసి ఆడవచ్చు: ఒక ఫోన్ ఆటను ఏర్పాటు చేస్తుంది, మిగతావారు ఆ ఫోన్ తెరపై ఉన్న QR కోడ్‌ను కెమెరాతో స్కాన్ చేసి చేరతారు. స్కాన్ తెర తెరిచి ఉన్నప్పుడు, ఆ QR కోడ్ చదవడానికి మాత్రమే కెమెరా వాడబడుతుంది; ఏ ఫోటో తీయబడదు, ఉంచబడదు, ఎక్కడికీ పంపబడదు. ఆట మీ వైఫైలోని ఫోన్ల మధ్యే నడుస్తుంది; మాకు ఏమీ రాదు. ఏర్పాటు చేసిన ఫోన్‌లో Android ఒక నోటిఫికేషన్ చూపిస్తూ ఆటను నేపథ్యంలో నడిపిస్తుంది; "ఏర్పాటు ఆపు" నొక్కినా, యాప్ మూసేసినా, 10 నిమిషాలు ఎవరూ ఆడకపోయినా ఏర్పాటు ఆగిపోతుంది.

నోటిఫికేషన్లు

మీరు అనుమతిస్తే, మధ్యలో ఆపిన ఆట గురించి మరుసటి రోజు గుర్తుచేస్తుంది, ఆన్‌లైన్ ఆటలో మీ వంతు వచ్చినప్పుడు తెలియజేస్తుంది. ఇవి మీ ఫోన్‌లోనే తయారవుతాయి; దీని కోసం ఏ సమాచారమూ ఎవరికీ పంపబడదు. సెట్టింగ్‌లలో వీటిని ఆపవచ్చు.

పిల్లలు

దేశీ బోర్డ్ గేమ్స్ పిల్లలకు తగినది. 13 ఏళ్లలోపు పిల్లలతో సహా ఎవరి నుండీ వ్యక్తిగత సమాచారాన్ని సేకరించదు. 18 ఏళ్లలోపు వారికి వ్యక్తిగతీకరించని ప్రకటనలు కనిపిస్తాయి.
